---
name: legal-docs
description: Use when drafting or changing Terms, Privacy Policy, consent text or data-handling documents.
---
- Mark every draft "Draft, not legal advice". Recommend review by a Bulgarian lawyer or GDPR specialist before launch.
- Version every document (for example 1.0, date). Consent records must store the version and the time of acceptance.
- Health data is a special category under GDPR. Say so plainly in the Privacy Policy.
- Keep the data inventory up to date: for each field, record what it is, why it is stored, where, how long, and who can see it.
- Include "This app does not replace 112" in the app and in every document.
- List every service that handles data (Supabase, Vercel, and any email or password-check service).
- Describe how a user can export or delete their data.
